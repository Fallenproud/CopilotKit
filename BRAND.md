# VIBELAB Brand Identity

## Brand Identity
VIBELAB is a modern, innovative platform that focuses on delivering high-quality digital solutions. Our mission is to empower users through technology and design.

## Color Palette
- **Primary Color:**
  - Hex: #4A90E2 
  - RGB: (74, 144, 226)

- **Secondary Color:**
  - Hex: #D0021B 
  - RGB: (208, 2, 27)

- **Accent Color:**
  - Hex: #F5A623 
  - RGB: (245, 166, 35)

## Typography System
- **Headings:**
  - Font Family: 'Roboto', sans-serif
  - Weight: Bold
  - Size: 24px
- **Body Text:**
  - Font Family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif
  - Weight: Regular
  - Size: 16px

## Branding Guidelines
1. **Logo Usage:**
   - Ensure there is sufficient clear space around the logo. 
   - Do not alter the logo colors.
   - Use the logo on a contrasting background.

2. **Voice and Tone:**
   - Use a friendly and approachable tone.
   - Maintain professional language in all communications.

3. **Imagery:**
   - Use high-quality images that reflect our core values.
   - Images should be relevant and relatable to our audience.

4. **Consistency:**
   - Maintain consistency in colors, fonts, and imagery across all platforms and materials.
   - Adhere to the guidelines to reinforce the brand identity.
